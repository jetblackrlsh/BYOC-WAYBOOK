Your character. Your approach. A new story.

Offline • Single character • Headless-ready

Bring your own character

Choose an adventure and imagine someone who belongs in it. Every approach uses the same simple rules.

Your description is flavor. It never changes the math. You cannot repeat the stat from your previous check.

Text command console

Commands: look, help, check mind Your approach, check body Your approach, check special Your approach, retry, restart, quit, save, log. File imports remain available above.



How this works
Roll d6 + the chosen stat + applicable circumstances. The combined modifier is capped at −2 to +2. A total of 4 or more succeeds. Both success and failure make that stat unavailable for your next check. Your personality determines the prompt you see.
Explicit retries use fresh randomness and keep earlier attempts in the log. Ordinary save/resume preserves upcoming random results. Export saves for reliable portability; local autosave availability depends on your browser.
